# Player Survey

**Tool:** Google Forms (free). Put a QR code linking to it on a sign at events.
**Length:** about 3 minutes, 14 questions.  **Target:** 30+ responses from 2–3 events.
**Answers:** RQ2, RQ3, RQ4, RQ6

Questions marked *(required)* should be set as required in Google Forms.

---

## Form description (paste at the top)

> Help make local Lorcana events better! This anonymous survey takes about 3 minutes.
> It's part of a project to build free tournament software for local stores.
> Your answers are only reported as totals, never individually.

## Section 1: About you

**1. How often do you play in Lorcana events?** *(required, multiple choice)*
- More than once a week
- About once a week
- A few times a month
- About once a month
- Less than once a month
- This is my first event

**2. How would you describe yourself as a player?** *(required, multiple choice)*

**7. How well do you understand how tiebreakers decide standings?** *(linear scale

---

## Analysis notes

- Questions 4–6 give baseline numbers to compare against after the pilot. Ask the
  same questions again at pilot events to measure change.
- Question 9 gives a simple feature ranking for prioritizing the backlog.
- Compare answers between casual and competitive players (question 2). They may
  want different things.
- Export responses as CSV. They can go into the database and Tableau with the
  event data later.
- Keep contact details from question 14 out of the exported data and out of git.
