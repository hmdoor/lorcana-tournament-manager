# Interview Guide: Players

**Length:** 20 minutes, or the 10-minute version (★ questions only) between rounds
**Participants:** a mix of casual and competitive Lorcana players
**Answers:** RQ2, RQ3, RQ4, RQ6

See the [organizer guide](interview-guide-organizers.md) for interviewing tips. The
same rules apply: ask about real past events, don't lead, and don't show the app
until the end.

## 1. Introduction and consent (2 min)

> I'm researching how local Lorcana tournaments work, to design software that makes
> them better for players and organizers. I'd like to ask about your experience.
> I'll take notes but won't use your name; you'll just be "Player 3" or similar.
> Is that OK? You can skip any question.

## 2. Background (3 min)

1. ★ How long have you played Lorcana? How often do you go to events?
2. ★ Which stores or events do you go to?
3. Would you describe yourself as more casual or more competitive? Why?
4. What decks or inks do you usually play?

## 3. Event experience (8 min)

5. ★ Think about the last event you went to. Walk me through it from arriving to
   leaving.
6. ★ How did you find out who you were playing and where to sit each round?
7. How long did you usually wait between rounds? What did you do while waiting?
8. Did you know your standing during the event? How did you find out?
9. Have you ever had a problem with a pairing, a result or the standings? What
   happened?
10. Do you understand how tiebreakers work? *(Note how confident they sound, not just
    yes or no.)*
11. ★ What's the most frustrating part of going to events?
12. What's the best-run event you've been to? What made it good?

## 4. Information and data (4 min)

13. After an event, what information do you look for? Where do you find it?
14. Do you track your own results or decks anywhere? How?
15. ★ If you could see stats about your own play, what would you want to know?
16. Would you want to see what decks or inks are winning at local events?

## 5. Privacy (2 min)

17. ★ How would you feel about your name and results appearing on a public standings
    page?
18. How about your deck or ink choices being recorded and shown in statistics?
    *(Probe: Does it matter if it's anonymous? Only after the event?)*

## 6. Wrap-up (1 min)

> If you could change one thing about local Lorcana events, what would it be? Would
> you be willing to fill out a short survey or test the app at an event?

Thank them.
